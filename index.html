Orbits use circular approximations of real semi-major axes, sidereal periods and J2000 mean longitudes — great for seeing relative motion and retrograde loops, not for precision ephemeris work. Distances are square-root scaled per panel so inner and outer planets both fit on screen, and the Moon's orbit is drawn at a fixed exaggerated radius so it stays visible next to Earth.
